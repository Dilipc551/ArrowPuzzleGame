How to play

  1. 1

    Read the arrow

    Every tile points up, down, left, or right. That is the only direction it can travel.

  2. 2

    Send it out

    Tap an arrow. If every square in front of it is empty, it slides off the board and is gone.

  3. 3

    If it shakes, wait

    Another arrow is in the way. It cannot jump over or pass through. Remove the one in front first.

  4. 4

    Clear the board

    When the last arrow leaves, the level is complete and the next one unlocks. Hint marks an arrow that can leave right now. Restart puts the current level back to the start. An arrow that leaves is worth 10 points, finishing the level adds 100, and a hint costs 20. The score stays at zero or above, and restarting starts that level's score over.

Keys

  • Arrow keys move between arrows
  • Enter releases the selected arrow
  • H hint · R restart · M mute
  • Esc goes back

Sound is optional. Use the speaker button in the corner. Your unlocked levels are saved in this browser only.
