# Arrow Escape

A browser puzzle. Arrows sit on a grid. Tap one to send it off the board. It can leave only when every square in front of it is empty. Clear the board to finish the level.

There are 12 levels. They get harder as you go. The next level unlocks when you clear the current one. Progress is saved in the browser you are using.

## Play on a MacBook

You do not need to install anything.

1. Open Finder.
2. Go to Downloads, then open the ArrowPuzzleGame folder.
3. Double-click `index.html`.

The game opens in your usual web browser (Safari, Chrome, or Firefox).

Use the same browser each time if you want to keep your unlocked levels. Safari and Chrome do not share saves. If a level you already cleared is locked again the next time you open the game, open `index.html` in Google Chrome instead.

Sound is off or on from the round speaker button in the top corner. The game is fully playable with sound muted.

## How to play

- Tap or click an arrow.
- If its path is clear, it slides away.
- If another arrow is in the way, it shakes and stays. Remove the one in front first.
- Hint highlights one arrow that can leave right now. Each hint costs 20 points. The score never goes below zero.
- Each arrow that leaves is worth 10 points. Clearing the level adds 100. A blocked arrow scores nothing.
- Restart puts only the current level back to the start and sets that level's score back to zero. It does not lock levels again, and it does not add points.
- Your best finished score is saved with your level progress.

On a keyboard: arrow keys move between arrows, Enter releases the selected arrow, H is hint, R is restart, M mutes sound, and Esc goes back.

## YouTube

Inside YouTube, sound follows YouTube's own volume control, and your level progress is saved with the game there. When you clear a level, your best score is sent to YouTube. The speaker button in the corner is for playing the file directly on your computer. The score still works there, without YouTube.

## Erase saved progress

Open the game, choose How to play, then Erase saved progress. Confirm the question. Only level 1 stays unlocked, and the best score returns to zero. This affects the browser you are using, not your whole computer.
